Three ways into Gmail, in order of reliability:

  1. Drag the preview above straight into the Gmail compose window.
  2. Copy image for Gmail, then Ctrl/Cmd + V in the message body. (Right-clicking the preview and choosing Copy image does the same thing, and works in browsers that block the button.)
  3. Download PNG, then use Gmail's Insert photo button, or drag the file in from your downloads.

Gmail discards images embedded in pasted HTML, so the picture has to arrive as a real image — which is what all three of these do.
